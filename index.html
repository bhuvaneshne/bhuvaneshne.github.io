About

At Highway9 Networks (private 4G/5G for enterprises) I lead core architecture for the edge platform: 4G/5G core, user plane, enterprise network integration, bare-metal and air-gapped deployments, and the radio side (CBRS, PTP timing, radio diagnostics). I took field deployments from frequent crashes to 6+ months of sustained uptime, introduced a Secure Web Gateway on the 4G edge, and lead root-cause analysis on field escalations that cross edge, radio and cloud.

Before that, ten years at Radisys as an architect, working on GTP load balancing, P4/OpenFlow packet engines, Software Defined PON, hyperscale DPDK EPC and 5G UPF, and five years at Tech Mahindra on LTE, UMA/GANC and CDMA. Outside work: app development, embedded systems, home automation, fishkeeping, automotive mechanics, motorcycles and cycling.
